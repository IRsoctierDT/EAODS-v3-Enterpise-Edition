# Enterprise EAODS Capability Maturity Model, Assessment Methodology & Certification Framework

## Purpose

This standard establishes the Enterprise EAODS Capability Maturity Model (ECMM), providing a structured methodology for assessing, benchmarking, certifying, and continuously improving organizational implementation of EAODS capabilities.

The framework transforms EAODS from a collection of standards into an objectively measurable enterprise operating system through standardized assessments, evidence-based scoring, governance oversight, and continuous improvement planning.

---

# Strategic Objectives

The framework shall:

- establish repeatable organizational assessments;
- measure capability maturity across all EAODS domains;
- standardize evidence-based scoring;
- support executive decision-making;
- benchmark organizational progress;
- enable structured certification;
- drive continual improvement.

---

# Assessment Principles

Assessments shall be:

- objective;
- evidence-based;
- repeatable;
- independently verifiable;
- risk-informed;
- governance-driven;
- transparent;
- continuously improved.

# Capability Assessment Criteria

Each capability shall be evaluated against:

- governance;
- implementation;
- operational effectiveness;
- automation;
- evidence quality;
- measurement;
- resilience;
- continuous improvement.

---

# Assessment Scoring Model

Every capability shall receive:

| Dimension | Weight |
|------------|--------|
| Governance | 20% |
| Technical Implementation | 20% |
| Operational Effectiveness | 20% |
| Evidence Quality | 15% |
| Automation | 10% |
| Continuous Assurance | 10% |
| Improvement Program | 5% |

Organizations may adjust weighting only through documented governance approval.

---

# Evidence Requirements

Assessments shall reference:

- approved policies;
- implemented controls;
- operational metrics;
- configuration evidence;
- validation reports;
- audit records;
- executive approvals;
- continuous assurance results.

Assertions without supporting evidence shall not contribute to maturity scores.

# Surveillance Assessment Program

Between full assessments, organizations shall conduct:

- quarterly control sampling;
- evidence completeness reviews;
- corrective action verification;
- maturity trend analysis;
- governance health reviews.

Material declines in maturity shall trigger reassessment.

---

# Corrective Action Governance

Every assessment finding shall define:

- finding identifier;
- affected capability;
- maturity impact;
- owner;
- remediation plan;
- target completion;
- verification evidence;
- closure approval.

Certification status shall reflect unresolved critical findings.

---

# Enterprise Case Study

## Scenario

A multinational enterprise has implemented EAODS across governance, AI operations, platform engineering, cybersecurity, and data management. Executive leadership seeks an objective mechanism to measure implementation quality, identify improvement priorities, and demonstrate governance maturity.

### Challenge

Existing assessments vary between departments, producing inconsistent scoring and limited executive visibility into enterprise capability maturity.

### EAODS Implementation

The Enterprise Capability Maturity Model introduces standardized assessment criteria, weighted scoring, evidence validation, surveillance assessments, and organizational certification. Results are linked to the Enterprise Knowledge Graph and presented through Executive Control Tower dashboards, enabling consistent benchmarking and measurable improvement planning.

### Outcome

The organization establishes repeatable capability assessments, improves executive oversight, strengthens governance accountability, aligns investments with measurable outcomes, and creates a defensible certification process supporting long-term EAODS adoption.
