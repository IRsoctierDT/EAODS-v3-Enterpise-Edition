# Enterprise Operating Model & Capability Framework
## Volume 3: Enterprise Organizational Design, Decision Rights, Federated Governance & Executive Accountability Architecture

# Purpose

This volume defines the enterprise governance operating model responsible for transforming strategy into accountable execution. It establishes organizational structures, executive committees, delegated authorities, governance boards, decision rights, escalation pathways, and accountability mechanisms that support the EAODS ecosystem.

The operating model is designed to provide distributed operational autonomy while preserving centralized governance, architectural consistency, cybersecurity oversight, and executive accountability.

---

# Strategic Objectives

The governance operating model shall:

- establish clear decision authority;
- eliminate governance ambiguity;
- improve executive accountability;
- strengthen cross-functional collaboration;
- integrate Domain 03 into enterprise leadership;
- accelerate informed decision-making;
- maintain traceable governance records.

---

# Governance Principles

Enterprise governance shall be:

- authority-based;
- policy-driven;
- evidence-supported;
- transparent;
- measurable;
- risk-informed;
- continuously reviewed;
- independently auditable.

# Enterprise Case Study

## Scenario

A multinational technology organization experiences delayed decision-making due to overlapping committees, inconsistent authority, and fragmented cybersecurity governance. Major AI initiatives require approvals from multiple groups without a clearly defined decision hierarchy.

### Challenge

Executive leadership seeks a governance model that reduces ambiguity while preserving strong oversight for cybersecurity, architecture, AI, and enterprise operations.

### EAODS Implementation

The organization implements the EAODS federated governance architecture, formalizes governance charters, defines delegated authority, and introduces a centralized decision register integrated with the Enterprise Knowledge Graph. Domain 03 becomes a standing participant in architecture reviews, portfolio governance, and operational risk discussions. Executive dashboards provide visibility into governance performance, approval timelines, and implementation outcomes.

### Outcome

Decision latency decreases, accountability becomes measurable, cybersecurity is embedded into strategic governance rather than operating independently, and executive leadership gains complete traceability from strategic objectives through implementation and operational performance.
