# Enterprise Case Study

## Scenario

A multinational critical infrastructure operator expands its autonomous AI workforce across engineering, finance, legal, cybersecurity, and executive decision support. Multiple governance frameworks exist, but authority boundaries have become inconsistent across regions and business units.

### Challenge

Executive leadership requires a single constitutional authority establishing immutable governance principles while allowing individual operating frameworks to evolve independently.

### EAODS Implementation

The Enterprise Digital Constitution becomes the supreme governance layer for every EAODS framework. Policy Intelligence validates constitutional compliance before operational authorization. The Enterprise Trust Fabric enforces delegated authority, while Continuous Assurance evaluates constitutional adherence across autonomous systems. Domain 03 is elevated from a technical function to a constitutional responsibility supporting enterprise resilience, identity assurance, and executive decision integrity.

### Outcome

The enterprise establishes a durable constitutional governance model that aligns strategy, architecture, cybersecurity, autonomous systems, and executive accountability under a unified operating charter. Subsequent EAODS frameworks evolve without compromising foundational governance principles.
