# EAODS v17.2
## Volume 6 — Enterprise Executive Cyber Crisis Management & Board Decision Playbook

# Purpose

This playbook establishes the executive operating model for managing enterprise cyber crises requiring strategic leadership, cross-functional coordination, and Board oversight.

Its purpose is to ensure that executive decisions remain deliberate, evidence-based, documented, and aligned with enterprise governance while preserving operational authority within Enterprise Cyber Command.

Executive governance directs strategy. Enterprise Cyber Command executes operations.

---

# Executive Mission

The Executive Cyber Crisis Management capability shall:

- preserve critical business capabilities;
- protect organizational trust;
- enable informed executive decisions;
- coordinate enterprise leadership;
- oversee strategic recovery;
- satisfy governance obligations;
- accelerate organizational learning.

---

# Executive Governance Principles

Executive crisis leadership shall remain:

- evidence-informed;
- business-capability focused;
- proportionate;
- transparent;
- accountable;
- constitutionally governed;
- independently reviewable;
- continuously documented.

Operational execution shall remain delegated unless strategic intervention becomes necessary.

# Executive Decision Matrix

Strategic decisions shall evaluate:

- business capability impact;
- operational dependencies;
- legal considerations;
- regulatory obligations;
- financial implications;
- reputational considerations;
- workforce safety;
- recovery confidence.

Decision rationale shall be preserved within the governance record.

---

# Executive Briefing Standard

Every executive briefing shall contain:

- verified operational facts;
- current business impacts;
- affected critical capabilities;
- strategic risks;
- available response options;
- known assumptions and uncertainties;
- executive decisions requested;
- next scheduled briefing.

Analytical judgments shall be clearly distinguished from confirmed observations.

---

# Board Governance Workflow

Board reporting shall summarize:

- enterprise operational posture;
- material business impacts;
- strategic risks;
- resilience status;
- governance actions;
- assurance observations;
- strategic recovery outlook.

The Board shall provide strategic oversight rather than direct operational management.

---

# Crisis Communications Governance

Communication shall be coordinated across:

- executive leadership;
- employees;
- customers;
- business partners;
- regulators where applicable;
- media where authorized.

Message approval authority shall be documented before external release.

# Legal & Regulatory Coordination

For each material incident, the organization shall perform documented legal and compliance review to determine:

- contractual notification obligations;
- applicable regulatory requirements;
- evidence preservation needs;
- litigation readiness considerations;
- executive approval requirements.

Jurisdiction-specific legal decisions shall remain the responsibility of qualified legal counsel.

# Enterprise Case Study

## Scenario

A multinational healthcare provider experiences simultaneous ransomware activity, cloud identity disruption, and degradation of patient scheduling systems across multiple regions.

### Challenge

Operational teams effectively execute technical response procedures, but executive leadership requires a structured governance model for coordinating legal review, communications, business continuity, strategic recovery priorities, and Board oversight.

### EAODS Implementation

The Executive Cyber Crisis Management Playbook activates the Cyber Crisis Management Team, establishes scheduled executive briefings, coordinates legal and continuity functions, documents strategic decisions, and synchronizes executive reporting through the Executive Control Tower. Continuous Assurance validates governance activities while Enterprise Cyber Command retains responsibility for technical execution.

### Outcome

The organization maintains disciplined executive governance throughout the crisis, aligns strategic recovery decisions with business priorities, improves cross-functional coordination, and captures governance lessons that strengthen future crisis preparedness.
