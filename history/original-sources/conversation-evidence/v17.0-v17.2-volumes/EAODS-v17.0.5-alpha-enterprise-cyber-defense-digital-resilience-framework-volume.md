# EAODS v17.0-alpha
## Volume 6: Enterprise Digital Forensics, Evidence Governance & Investigative Assurance Framework

# Purpose

The Enterprise Digital Forensics Platform (EDFP) establishes the authoritative framework for acquiring, preserving, validating, analyzing, governing, and presenting digital evidence across enterprise cybersecurity investigations.

The framework ensures investigative activities produce reliable, reproducible, and independently verifiable evidence supporting operational decisions, regulatory obligations, litigation readiness, executive governance, and institutional learning.

Evidence shall remain a governed enterprise asset throughout its lifecycle.

---

# Strategic Objectives

The Enterprise Digital Forensics Platform shall:

- establish enterprise investigative governance;
- preserve evidentiary integrity;
- strengthen Domain 03 investigative maturity;
- improve executive confidence in investigative findings;
- support legally defensible evidence handling;
- institutionalize forensic knowledge;
- enable continuous investigative assurance.

---

# Investigative Principles

Enterprise investigations shall remain:

- evidence-driven;
- reproducible;
- minimally intrusive;
- independently reviewable;
- constitutionally governed;
- fully documented;
- proportionate to mission objectives;
- traceable from acquisition through disposition.

Investigative conclusions shall distinguish observed facts, analytical findings, and professional judgments.

# Enterprise Case Study

## Scenario

A multinational healthcare provider experiences a sophisticated compromise affecting cloud identity services, electronic health record integrations, and administrative systems.

### Challenge

Executive leadership requires rapid forensic analysis while preserving evidence integrity, supporting regulatory obligations, coordinating legal review, and informing enterprise recovery.

### EAODS Implementation

The Enterprise Digital Forensics Platform registers every evidence item through the Chain-of-Custody Framework, acquires cloud and endpoint artifacts using standardized laboratory procedures, and correlates findings with Threat Intelligence, Detection Engineering, and Incident Response through the Enterprise Knowledge Graph. AI-assisted tooling accelerates artifact indexing and timeline reconstruction, while human forensic analysts validate conclusions prior to executive reporting. Continuous Assurance independently evaluates investigative quality before case closure.

### Outcome

The organization completes a technically rigorous and governance-aligned investigation that preserves evidentiary integrity, supports executive decision-making, improves future detection content, and strengthens Domain 03 investigative maturity through documented organizational learning.
