# Purpose

The Cyber Defense Operating System (CDOS) establishes the operational architecture governing enterprise cybersecurity execution across prevention, detection, investigation, response, recovery, intelligence, engineering, resilience, and continuous improvement.

CDOS provides the authoritative operating model for Domain 03 by integrating governance, autonomous assistance, human expertise, telemetry, intelligence, and executive oversight into a unified cyber defense capability.

The Cyber Defense Operating System shall:

- establish a unified Domain 03 operating model;
- continuously reduce enterprise cyber risk;
- improve detection and response effectiveness;
- institutionalize cyber resilience;
- enable governed AI-assisted security operations;
- preserve evidence integrity;
- maintain executive cyber situational awareness.

# Enterprise Case Study

## Scenario

A multinational financial services enterprise operates hybrid infrastructure, cloud-native applications, AI-enabled customer platforms, and a globally distributed Security Operations Center.

### Challenge

The organization must unify cyber operations across regional teams while improving resilience, integrating AI-assisted investigations, and preserving constitutional governance over autonomous security activities.

### EAODS Implementation

CDOS establishes standardized operating procedures, a common telemetry fabric, governed detection engineering, intelligence-driven investigations, and centralized cyber mission governance. Autonomous analytical capabilities assist investigators under delegated authority while every operational decision remains traceable through the Enterprise Knowledge Graph. Continuous Assurance validates operational effectiveness, telemetry integrity, and evidence quality.

### Outcome

The enterprise transitions from fragmented cybersecurity operations to a unified, measurable, intelligence-driven cyber defense capability. Executive leadership gains real-time visibility into cyber resilience, operational maturity, investigation quality, and strategic cyber readiness while maintaining governance, accountability, and continuous improvement.
