Enterprise AI Security Operations Reference Architecture & Operating Model

Purpose

This standard establishes the Enterprise AI Security Operations Center (AI-SOC) operating model that unifies governance, threat intelligence, detection engineering, response automation, incident command, cyber recovery, resilience validation, and AI orchestration into a single enterprise operating architecture.

The AI-SOC serves as the operational realization of EAODS, providing a governed execution model where humans and AI agents collaborate through policy-driven workflows supported by continuous evidence generation and executive oversight.

⸻

Strategic Objectives

The AI-SOC shall:

* unify operational cybersecurity capabilities;
* establish standardized human and AI responsibilities;
* enable policy-driven automation;
* improve operational scalability;
* reduce response latency;
* provide executive operational visibility;
* support measurable cyber resilience.

⸻

Enterprise Operating Principles

Operations shall be:

* governance-first;
* evidence-driven;
* policy-authorized;
* human-accountable;
* continuously observable;
* AI-assisted;
* resilient by design;
* continuously improved.
